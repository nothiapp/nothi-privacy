Delete Your Nothi Account and Data

App: Nothi – Notification History
Developer: Eren
Contact: nothiapp@gmail.com

Nothi saves your notifications on your device. They are uploaded only if you turn on Cloud Sync, which creates a Cloud Sync account linked to your Google account.

Delete your Cloud Sync account and cloud data

In the app

1. Open Nothi and go to Settings
2. In Cloud Sync, tap Delete cloud data & account and confirm
3. Choose the Google account you used for Cloud Sync

This works with or without Premium. Deletion is immediate.

Without the app

Email nothiapp@gmail.com from the Google account you used, with the subject "Delete my Nothi data". We delete the account and all its synced data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Delete data on your device

In the app: long-press a notification in the feed, tap Select all, then Delete
Or: device Settings → Apps → Nothi → Storage → Clear data
Or simply uninstall the app — all data on the device is removed permanently

What is deleted

  • All notifications synced to the cloud (Google Firebase Cloud Firestore)
  • Your Cloud Sync account (Firebase Authentication record with your Google account ID and email)
  • On-device deletion: notification history, app preferences and settings

Nothing from the deleted Cloud Sync account is kept afterwards.

What is not deleted

  • Notifications saved on your device, when you delete only the cloud account
  • Crash reports already sent to Firebase Crashlytics (kept up to 90 days by Google). They contain no notification content.
  • Analytics events already sent to Firebase Analytics (kept up to 14 months by Google). They contain no notification content.
  • Google Play purchase records (managed by Google, not us)
