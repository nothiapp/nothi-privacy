Privacy Policy

App: Nothi – Notification History
Package: com.nothi.app
Effective date: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what data the Nothi: Notification History Android app ("Nothi", "the app") handles, where it goes, and how you can delete it. "We" and "us" refer to the developer of Nothi.

1. Notifications you capture

Nothi saves the notifications your phone receives so you can find them later. To do this it needs Android's Notification access permission, which you grant yourself in system settings and can revoke at any time.

For each notification Nothi stores: the app name and package name, title, text, expanded text, time, a category the app assigns, links found in the text, and whether the notification contained a one-time code (OTP). OTP codes themselves are never written to storage; Nothi only shows them in a temporary alert while the notification is fresh.

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. It is not encrypted by Nothi beyond the protection Android itself gives app storage.

Nothi does not send your notifications anywhere unless you turn on Cloud Sync (section 2).

2. Cloud Sync (optional, Premium)

Cloud Sync is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings and sign in with your Google account:

  • The notification data described in section 1 (except OTP codes) is uploaded to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and stored under your Google account, so you can restore it on another device.
  • Firebase Authentication receives your Google account identifier and email address to sign you in.
  • Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection.

You can turn Cloud Sync off and sign out at any time. To delete the data already uploaded, see section 6.

3. Analytics, performance and crash reports

Nothi uses these Google Firebase services on every install:

  • Firebase Analytics — which screens and features are used (for example a category name you selected, the length of a search, the plan you viewed). Search text and notification content are never sent.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring — app start time, screen rendering and network performance.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports with technical details (device model, Android version, stack trace). Crash reports do not include notification content.

These services also collect device and app identifiers (such as the Firebase installation ID) as described in Google's policies.

4. Ads (free plan only)

The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device information to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Premium users do not see ads.

5. Purchases

Premium subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We receive the purchase status needed to unlock Premium; we never receive your payment details.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: notifications are kept for the retention period you choose in Settings and within the storage limit of your plan. You can delete individual notifications or all of them in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on the device.
  • In the cloud (Cloud Sync): see "Delete your account and cloud data" below.

Delete your account and cloud data

Deleting your Cloud Sync account permanently removes all notifications synced to the cloud and the account itself. Nothing is kept afterwards. Notifications saved on your device are not affected.

  • In the app: open Settings → Cloud Sync → Delete cloud data & account, confirm, and choose the Google account you used. This works with or without Premium.
  • Without the app: email nothiapp@gmail.com from the Google account you used, with the subject "Delete my Nothi data". We delete the account and all its synced data within 30 days and confirm by email.

More details: Data deletion.

7. Permissions

  • Notification access — to read and save incoming notifications (section 1).
  • Post notifications — to show OTP alerts, Smart Alerts and app reminders.
  • Ignore battery optimizations (optional) — so Android does not stop the capture service in the background.
  • Internet / network state — for Cloud Sync, analytics, ads and purchases.

8. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the Google services named above, which process it on our behalf under their own terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

9. Children

Nothi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
